# Production exceptions — 2026-11-21

Decision source: human requester, 2026-11-21.

The following finance/tax and COD operating items are **accepted business exceptions** for the
production release. They are removed from the technical launch-blocker list, but they are **not**
recorded as completed controls. Statutory and financial obligations remain with the business and
must be operated manually until the system work is completed.

These exceptions do not authorize production release by themselves. `D-020-12` in
`production-transition-decision-pack.md` is still required for the exact release candidate.

## 1. Accepted exceptions

| ID | Exception | Risk accepted | Manual control | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EX-01 | TCS uses the 18% interim default until product GST rates exist | Exempt or differently-rated goods will over- or under-collect TCS | Finance reviews the COD statement monthly; flags exempt/differently-rated lines manually; corrects before GSTR-8 | Finance |
| EX-02 | Fee invoice, GSTR-8 export and automated reconciliation are incomplete | Invoices and filings are produced outside the platform and can drift | Accounting raises fee invoices and GSTR-8 from the dues CSV/statement; monthly reconciliation signed by Finance | Finance |
| EX-03 | CA sign-off on agency/TCS treatment is deferred | Tax position may be challenged | Written CA opinion obtained before the first statutory filing; Finance retains the risk | Finance / Legal |
| EX-04 | COD dues recovery, refunds, delivery-fee ownership and seller policies are manual | Unpaid dues, disputed refunds or delivery-fee leakage | Monthly dues statement; UPI/NEFT collection; COD disabled at 14 days and listings blocked at 30; seller credit note for refused goods; delivery fee follows the written policy | Finance / Operations |

## 2. Operating conditions

### TCS

- The COD ledger already records TCS at 0.5% of the GST-exclusive value using the 18% default.
- The statement exposes `tcs_default_rate_applied` and `tcs_pending`, and the admin COD page can
  export the period.
- Finance must review the period before filing and adjust for exempt/differently-rated products.
- No GSTR-8 file is generated by the platform; it is produced outside the system.

### Fee invoice and reconciliation

- The platform does not yet generate a Bazarbee fee invoice or a GSTR-8 export.
- Accounting produces them from the seller dues statement and the COD collections export.
- A monthly reconciliation must tie commission, MDR (prepaid only), refunds, TCS and dues to the
  ledger before the books are closed.

### COD dues and refunds

- Sellers collect cash; Bazarbee carries a receivable.
- The recovery waterfall remains: next prepaid payout, then monthly dues statement, then COD
  disabled at 14 days and listings blocked at 30.
- COD refunds are seller credit notes, not Bazarbee refunds.
- Delivery-fee forfeiture and refusal handling follow the written seller policy and legal review.

### Seller policies

- Six staging sellers are opted in with 3% commission.
- Production seller policies must be reviewed and approved before the first COD order.

## 3. Effect on release gates

- These items no longer block the technical release checklist.
- They **do not** mark G3, G6, G7, G8 or the finance/data-integrity cross-cutting gate as `pass`.
- The release record must list them as accepted exceptions with the owner and review date above.
- `D-020-12` still requires Release Authority, Executive Sponsor and Security approval.

## 4. Remaining production blockers

Not affected by this exception:

- production identity and Cloudflare Access/IdP for admin/seller/ERP
- Flutter production build and store signing
- Razorpay live keys and durable webhook
- production infrastructure, secrets and DNS/TLS
- backup/DR for all databases and volumes with a restore drill
- security assurance, observability and UAT
- legal review of the six policy pages
- data bootstrap from approved master data
