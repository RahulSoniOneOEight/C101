# Shipping and Delivery Policy

---

## 1. Who delivers your order

**Bazarbee** is a marketplace. Delivery is performed by the **Seller** who supplies
the goods, or by a delivery partner engaged by that Seller.

We do not operate a delivery fleet and do not make a platform-wide delivery promise.
Each Seller publishes its own serviceable pincodes, delivery fee and delivery
timeline, and those are shown to you at checkout before payment.

---

## 2. Serviceable pincodes

- Every Seller defines the pincodes it can serve.
- Enter your pincode on any page to see whether the item can be delivered to you.
- If your pincode is not serviceable, checkout will not be offered for that Seller's
  items. You may remove those items and order the rest.
- Serviceability can change. We show the current position at the time you order.

---

## 3. Delivery timeline

| Stage | What you see |
|---|---|
| Product page | The Seller's indicative dispatch window |
| Checkout | The Seller's committed delivery window for your pincode |
| Order confirmation | The same commitment, per Seller |

A cart with items from more than one Seller will have **one delivery charge per
Seller**, shown as a single total at checkout, and may arrive in separate shipments
on different dates.

Delays caused by weather, transport disruption, local restrictions or events beyond
a Seller's control are notified to you; you may cancel before dispatch for a full
refund.

---

## 4. Delivery charges

- Set by each Seller per pincode.
- Shown at checkout **before** payment. No delivery charge is added after payment
  is initiated.
- A Seller may offer free delivery above a threshold; the threshold applies to that
  Seller's items only.
- The delivery charge is inclusive of GST.

---

## 5. Cash on Delivery (COD)

- Available up to **₹20,000** per order, where the Seller offers COD and the address
  is serviceable.
- COD may be unavailable for certain products (for example, non-returnable bulk
  materials), certain pincodes, or accounts with a history of refusals.
- Please keep the exact amount ready. Delivery partners may not be able to give
  change.
- A delivery OTP may be required to complete handover.

---

## 6. Delivery attempt and refusal

- The delivery partner will attempt delivery at the address provided. Please ensure
  someone is available and the number is reachable.
- If delivery fails because the address is incorrect, unavailable, or the order is
  refused, the goods return to the Seller. Goods are refunded; the delivery fee is
  retained because the trip has been made.
- Repeated refusals may result in COD being withdrawn for that account.

---

## 7. Inspection on delivery

Please inspect the goods at the time of delivery:

- Check the quantity, grade and packaging against the invoice.
- Note any visible damage on the delivery document before signing.
- Report damage, shortage or wrong items within **48 hours**, with photographs.

For heavy or bulky materials, please ensure adequate access, space and labour for
unloading. Charges for failed delivery due to site access are the customer's.

---

## 8. Risk and title

Risk in the goods passes to you on delivery or on collection, as applicable. Title
passes on full payment.

---

## 9. Tracking

Where the Seller provides tracking, status is shown on the order page. Sellers who
do not provide live tracking will update the order status at dispatch and delivery.
