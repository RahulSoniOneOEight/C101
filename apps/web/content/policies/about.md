## Who we are

**Bazarbee** is an online marketplace for construction materials, hardware, sanitary
ware, electrical goods and tools, operated by **Nihar Jain** from Banswara,
Rajasthan.

We connect buyers — homeowners, contractors, site engineers and businesses — with
independent sellers who hold the stock and fulfil the order.

---

## How the marketplace works

1. **You order** on the Platform. Prices are in INR and include GST.
2. **The Seller supplies** the goods and issues the tax invoice.
3. **We collect payment** on the Seller's behalf — online, or cash on delivery up to
   ₹20,000 where the Seller offers it.
4. **The Seller delivers**, within the timeline and to the pincodes shown at
   checkout.
5. **We handle** the platform, payment processing, complaints and the grievance
   process.

The contract of sale is between you and the Seller. We operate the marketplace and
collect payment as the Seller's agent.

## Where we operate

We are based in Banswara, Rajasthan. Delivery coverage depends on the Seller and the

Coverage is expanding as more Sellers join.
