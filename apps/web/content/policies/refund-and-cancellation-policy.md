This policy forms part of our **Terms of Service**. It applies to all orders placed

## 1. Who handles your refund

The **Seller** is the supplier of the goods and funds refunds. We (the Platform)
process the refund on the Seller's behalf through the original payment channel or by
bank transfer for cash-on-delivery orders.

---

## 2. Cancellation by you

| When you cancel | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Before dispatch** | Full refund, including any delivery fee |
| **After dispatch, before delivery** | Goods refunded; the delivery fee is retained because the trip has started |
| **Refusal at the door** | Order treated as a return to origin; goods refunded, delivery fee retained |
| **After delivery** | Treated as a return under §3, not a cancellation |

To cancel, email **support@gobazarbee.com** with your order number as early as possible.
Cancellation is confirmed only when we reply — a dispatch may already be in progress.

## 3. Returns

You may request a return where the goods are:

- damaged in transit,
- different from what you ordered (wrong item, size, grade or quantity), or
- missing from the delivery.

**Report within 48 hours of delivery** with your order number and photographs of the
goods, packaging and invoice. We will arrange a replacement or a refund after the
Seller inspects the claim.

### Not returnable

- Cement, sand, aggregates and other bulk materials once dispatched
- Cut, sized or mixed materials (cut plywood, mixed paint, cut pipe)
- Opened chemicals, adhesives and sealants
- Customised or made-to-order items
- Goods damaged after delivery or used contrary to instructions

These exclusions reflect hygiene, safety and resale realities, not an attempt to
avoid liability for defective goods. Where goods are defective, your statutory
rights apply regardless of this list.

- **The delivery fee follows the refunded line.** If one of several lines is
  refunded, a proportional share of that Seller's delivery fee is refunded with it.
  If delivery was attempted, the fee is retained.
- Where a free-delivery threshold was met only because of an item that is later
  refunded, the delivery fee that would otherwise have applied may be deducted.
- Refunds are not issued in cash for prepaid orders.

## 5. Replacements

Where a replacement is agreed, it is dispatched by the Seller within the timeline
shown at checkout. If the replacement is unavailable, a refund is issued instead.

## 6. Cancellation by us or the Seller

We may cancel an order for unserviceability, stock unavailability, pricing error,
suspected fraud, or a breach of the Terms. Where payment was made, it is refunded in
full, including the delivery fee.

## 7. Delays caused by the Seller

If the Seller cannot deliver within the committed timeline, you may cancel before
dispatch for a full refund. We will contact you with the option to wait or cancel.

## 8. How to raise a claim

Email **support@gobazarbee.com** with:

1. Order number
2. Item(s) affected
3. Description of the problem
4. Photographs, where relevant

We acknowledge within **48 hours** and aim to resolve within **30 days**. If you are
not satisfied, escalate to the grievance officer named in the **Contact and
Grievance Policy**.

## 9. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019,
including your right to approach a consumer commission.
