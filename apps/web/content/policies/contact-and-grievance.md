## 5. If you are not satisfied

1. Reply to our resolution email, marking it **"Escalation"**.
2. The Grievance Officer reviews and responds within **15 days**.
3. If the matter remains unresolved, you may approach:
   - the **National Consumer Helpline** (consumerhelpline.gov.in), or
   - the appropriate **Consumer Commission** under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, or
   - the **Data Protection Board of India** for data-protection matters.

Nothing here limits your statutory rights.

## 6. Abuse of the complaint process

We will not act on complaints that are abusive, fraudulent, or intended to obtain
goods or refunds without basis. Repeated false claims may result in suspension of
the account, without affecting genuine statutory rights.
