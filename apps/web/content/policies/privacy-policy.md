## 3. Why we use it

We process personal data to:

1. create and manage your account;
2. accept, process, deliver and support orders;
3. collect payment and issue receipts and refunds;
4. share the minimum necessary details with the Seller and the delivery partner so
   your order can be fulfilled;
5. prevent fraud, verify identity and secure the Platform;
6. meet legal, tax and accounting obligations;
7. respond to complaints and legal requests; and
8. improve our catalogue, search and usability, using aggregated or de-identified
   data where possible.

We rely on your **consent** for marketing communications and optional features, and
on the **legitimate uses** permitted by law for the purposes above. You may withdraw
consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, nor
our ability to comply with legal obligations.

## 5. Payments

Prepaid payments are processed by our payment gateway partner. Your payment
instrument details are entered in the gateway's interface and are not transmitted to
or stored by us. We receive only a payment reference, status and amount.

Cash-on-delivery orders record that cash was collected, together with the delivery
reference.

## 6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in, remember your cart and pincode, and
protect against fraud. With your consent, we may use analytics cookies to understand
how the Platform is used. You can control cookies in your browser; disabling
essential cookies may break checkout.

## 7. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account and order records | For as long as your account is active, then as required for tax, accounting and dispute purposes (typically up to 8 years) |
| Payment and invoice records | As required by tax and accounting law |
| Support and complaint records | Up to 3 years after resolution |
| Marketing consent records | Until withdrawn, plus a short audit period |
| Server and security logs | Up to 12 months, unless needed for an investigation |

We delete or anonymise data when it is no longer needed for these purposes.

---

## 8. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, least-privilege roles, audit logging
and regular review of dependencies. No system is perfectly secure; if a personal
data breach occurs that affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority
as required by law.

## 9. Your rights

As a Data Principal you may:

- **access** a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed;
- **correct** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **erase** data that is no longer necessary, subject to legal retention duties;
- **withdraw consent** for processing based on consent;
- **nominate** another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or
  incapacity; and
- **complain** to us, and thereafter to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

To exercise any right, email **support@gobazarbee.com** with your request and a means of
verifying your identity. We respond within the timelines required by law.
