PRODUCT FLOW — UI / UX CHANGES (CHG-086)
Admin + Seller, staging. Date: 2026-10-27
Scope: what an operator sees and does. Backend behaviour is listed only
where it changes the screen.

======================================================================
CHANGE 1 — ADD NEW PRODUCT: NO CATALOGUE SEARCH
======================================================================
Before
  The create page opened with a "Catalogue search" card: a title search,
  a result list, and "Edit in studio / Open" links. It pushed the actual
  form below the fold and made create feel like a search task.

After
  The catalogue-search block is removed from /products/new.
  Create begins directly with the workspace cards.
  Nothing is pre-filled from another record; operators who need to find an
  existing product use Products in the sidebar or the Products list.

Result
  One less decision, one less scroll, and a clear start: "fill in the
  product, save it".

======================================================================
CHANGE 2 — ONE WORKSPACE FOR CREATE AND EDIT
======================================================================
- Create and Edit use the same component and the same layout.
- Same five destinations, same cards, same labels on both Admin and Seller.
- No second, differently-shaped form for new products.
- Create ends by navigating into the new product's workspace, so the
  operator continues in the same mental model.

======================================================================
CHANGE 3 — STICKY WORKSPACE NAVIGATION
======================================================================
- Five destinations, always reachable:
    Overview | Variants & offers (n) | Inventory | Activity |
    Review & visibility
- Counts are meaningful: variants/offers, inventory attention, blockers.
- A blocked destination shows an icon + text + count, never colour only:
    "Review & visibility · 1 blocker"
- Selecting a destination keeps it in the URL (?view=...), so Back and
  Forward work and a link can be shared.
- Mobile: the five tabs collapse into one labelled dropdown
  ("Workspace ▾"), one tap to any destination.
- The navigation stays pinned while the page scrolls.

======================================================================
CHANGE 4 — EXPANDABLE SECTION CARDS
======================================================================
- Every section is a collapsible card with a chevron, aria-expanded and a
  visible keyboard focus ring.
- New product: only "Product details" starts open. Search tags and
  Specifications start collapsed.
- Existing product: the operator's last open/closed state is restored for
  that session; stale state is ignored on create.
- Collapsed cards still say what is inside:
    "Needs title and category", "Optional", "5 values",
    "6 variants", "4/6 priced", "1 blocker · 3 warnings"
- Several cards may stay open at once, so values can be compared.
- A blocked card keeps its error summary visible when collapsed.
- Review "Fix" expands the right card and focuses the exact field, not
  just the top of the page.

======================================================================
CHANGE 5 — OVERVIEW CARD CONTENT
======================================================================
Product details
- Product title, short description, full description.
- Category selector.
- Selling channels, read-only: shows the assigned channel badges, or
  "No sales channel assigned". Explains that publication is lifecycle and
  visibility also needs price + stock.
- Admin only: optional seller-offer provisioning with a required reason.
- Product type, brand, manufacturer, model/part number, HSN/GST code,
  unit of measure, thumbnail URL.
- Live content preview on the right (desktop) with title, image, tags,
  variant/SKU count, offer price and specifications.
- Readiness rail on the right lists content checks (title, category, tags,
  specs, variants, pricing, thumbnail).

Search tags
- Chips with remove buttons, Enter/comma to add, suggestions from existing
  tags, Backspace removes the last one.

Specifications
- One row per spec: name, value, optional unit.
- Add/remove rows, suggestion list, and a running
  "n complete · n incomplete" counter.

======================================================================
CHANGE 6 — VARIANTS & OFFERS
======================================================================
Order on the page
- The offer table now comes FIRST. Variant editing comes second.
- Reason: pricing and visibility are the daily tasks; variant editing is
  occasional.

Offer table
- Columns: select checkbox, Variant / SKU, Seller, B2C price, B2B status,
  Open.
- Missing B2C price is written as "⛔ Missing", not left blank.
- B2B shows the saved amount or "Not set".

Focused offer editor
- One offer at a time, with "Offer pricing · 2 of 6" context.
- Previous / Next / Close stay in the header.
- One explicit "Save pricing" action for that offer only.
- B2C: list price, selling price, GST-inclusive, live discount readout.
- B2B: list, selling, MOQ, effective dates, quantity tiers.
- B2C and B2B save independently; a B2B failure keeps its inputs and the
  action becomes a retry, without re-saving B2C.
- Closing or moving away with unsaved pricing warns instead of discarding.

Bulk editing
- Bulk controls appear only after rows are selected.
- The panel states: "Bulk edit · 2 selected offers".
- The channel is explicit. B2B is deliberately not a bulk field (MOQ,
  tiers and dates are per offer).
- The action reads "Apply to 2", not "Apply to all offers".

Variants
- Direct variant rows: name, SKU, details shown in the app, image URL.
- Duplicate / Remove per row; Add variant for editable drafts.
- Locked products say why: published or has offers.
- Bulk SKU prefix and "Fill blank SKUs" are hidden when the product is
  locked, instead of showing disabled leftovers.

Legacy clutter
- Legacy option axes no longer appear as editable cards or compatibility
  warnings.
- Admin only: a collapsed "Technical: legacy option axes (n)" drawer keeps
  the record inspectable without putting it in the workflow.

======================================================================
CHANGE 7 — SAVING THAT MATCHES THE SCOPE
======================================================================
- Three clearly separate scopes:
    Product content     -> "Save changes"
    One seller offer    -> "Save pricing"
    Selected bulk rows  -> "Apply to n"
- Inventory never uses "Save": it is "Request adjustment" / "Submit
  request", and says it does not change Tryton stock.
- The product save bar is sticky only while the product is dirty; when
  clean there is no bar taking up space.
- Wording is explicit: "Unsaved product changes · affects content, not
  pricing", "Saving product changes…", "Saved to server".
- Ctrl/Cmd+S saves the product scope and suppresses the browser dialog.
- Discard is available on the dirty bar.
- Leaving or refreshing with unsaved work triggers a native warning; the
  dialog names the dirty scope.
- Local autosave is labelled a recovery copy, never "saved".

======================================================================
CHANGE 8 — REVIEW & VISIBILITY
======================================================================
Header
- Record status and channel status are separate.
- Auto-published but not sellable shows: "Published · Not live".
- Per-channel badges: "B2C live / B2C · Not live",
  "B2B live / B2B · Not live".

Body
- Server-checked timestamp, blocker count and warning count.
- Per channel, the exact gates are spelled out:
    Published ✓ · Offer ✓ · Price ✓ · Stock ✕
- The blocker is written in words, e.g.
    "Priced seller offers have no sellable stock."
    "No active B2B price is saved for this seller offer."
- Every actionable item has a Fix button that lands on the owning view and
  field.
- After a fix, "Back to Review" and a refresh of server readiness.
- Warnings (image, tags, B2B terms) are visually distinct from blockers.
- Content checks remain as a secondary list, not the primary story.
- Lifecycle actions are disabled while product or pricing is dirty, with
  the reason shown next to them.

======================================================================
CHANGE 9 — INVENTORY AND ACTIVITY
======================================================================
Inventory
- Read-only Tryton position per SKU: on hand, reserved, ATP, draft moves.
- Location table when there are movements.
- If Tryton has nothing: a plain statement, not an error.
- Adjustment request form with quantity, reason and submit; last three
  requests shown.
- Footer states Tryton remains the authority.

Activity
- New destination listing audited product, lifecycle and seller changes,
  newest first, with actor and status.

======================================================================
CHANGE 10 — MOBILE
======================================================================
- Single column; no clipped tables.
- One sticky bottom bar, and only while something is dirty.
- Destination dropdown replaces the tab row.
- Offer detail is full-width with Back / Previous / Next.
- Touch targets at least 44 px.
- Save and error messages announced with aria-live.

======================================================================
CHANGE 11 — ACCESSIBILITY AND TONE
======================================================================
- Expand/collapse is a real button with aria-expanded.
- Current destination uses aria-current.
- Errors pair colour with an icon and text; red is reserved for blockers.
- Focus moves to the field a Fix link targets.
- Labels say what will happen ("Save changes", "Apply to 2", "Request
  adjustment") rather than a generic "Save".
- No unexplained counters: "Variants & offers (6)" and "1 blocker" replace
  the old "3/5 sections ready".
