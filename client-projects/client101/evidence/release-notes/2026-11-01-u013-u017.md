# Release note — 2026-11-01 (U-013, U-014, U-015, U-017)

**Environment:** staging only · production untouched
**Audience:** pilot stakeholders, support, QA

## What shipped

| Update | Customer-facing change |
|---|---|
| **U-013 — price units** | B2C selling prices and quantity tiers now save the amount the operator types. IPHONE 17 is published at **₹1,85,000** (Black) and **₹1,95,000** (White) against a ₹2,00,000 list, instead of the ₹1,850 / ₹1,950 artefact. A confirmation asks before saving a price below 10% of the list. |
| **U-014 — option integrity** | IPHONE 17 (Black/White) and Sumsan Fold 7 (Blue/Black/White) now carry real Color options, linked variant values and ordered ranks. The Product Studio no longer shows a “read-only” dead end, and the section hint reads “Each variant needs a unique SKU”. |
| **U-015 — Smoke cleanup** | The three allowlisted test products (Smoke 134657, 241288, 594475) are archived and no longer appear in the catalogue. |
| **U-017 — product page** | New 3–4 image gallery (swipe, dots, thumbnails), short-detail line, price/availability block, **Discuss** as a secondary action under Add/Buy, sticky Add/Buy bar, and price comparison moved below the specifications. |

Also live from the previous release: U-011 Browse layout (0.64 tiles, duplicate strip removed, unpinned filter bar), B2B chat parity, QC fixes 1–8, U-007 tile.
