## 2. What the run proves

- The **delivery fee is resolved before placing** and is the same number the
  order carries — no estimate, no client-side fee table.
- The **fee is invalidated** when the basket changes (qty 1 → 2 drops it back to
  “Calculated at checkout”) and re-resolved on the next checkout entry.
- A **site change / cart return reuses** the prepared cart (step 8 shows the fee
  without a new site selection).
- The **discount row** is required for honest arithmetic; it now appears in the
  cart, checkout and confirmation.

## 3. Notes and findings

1. **Add from the trade PDP routes to the quotation cart.** The B2B PDP has no
   toast; the cart is the confirmation. Different from B2C (toast + stay), and
   intentional in the current design. Screenshot `b2b_qc_01_pdp.png`.
2. **B2B order history is local-only (open item).** `B2BOrdersScreen`
   (`apps/prototype_app/lib/screens/b2b_shell.dart:673`) renders
   `b2bOrdersProvider`, which is persisted on the device, not fetched from the
   API. The order is real server-side (completion payload, step 13) but a new
   device/session will not list past business orders. Recorded in the release
   register.
3. **Six QC/test products remain searchable in B2C** (`QC Auto Publish 2610`,
   `QC CHG-086 272555`, `QC Import 124539`, `QC Import 124657`,
   `QC Studio 127088`, `QC CSV 127088`) — already recorded; still blocked on an
   administrator session + DB backup.
